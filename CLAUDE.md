## Rules
- Topic details live in the folder's own `CLAUDE.md`; keep this file short.
- When starting work in a folder, read its `CLAUDE.md` first, and update it at the end of the session.
- Log files (like `scalar/LOG.md`) are append-only history; open them only when history is needed.
- Personal preferences / persona live in the user-level `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` (not in this repo).
- **Gita sloka ritual (Sai's request, Oct 15):** start, middle (at a natural beat, e.g. stuck or a solved task) and end of each session, share one Bhagavad Gita sloka or phrase fitted to the moment, with a one-line meaning tied to the task, in the usual cheeky voice. Only cite verse numbers when sure. Always add a short plain-English "in simple words" explanation of the verse (Sai's request, Oct 15). Full detail in the user-level file, section 4.
