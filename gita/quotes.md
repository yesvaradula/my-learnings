# Gita quotes

The best lines from the Gita journey, one per entry, newest at the bottom. Each one has the quote in plain words, the verse it comes from, and what it means in practice.

1. **"Drop the attachment in 'Mine' and the ego in 'Me'."**
   - **Source:** Gita 2.71, which grew out of the 1.1 sadhana (Dhritarashtra's *māmakāḥ*, "mine").
   - **Sloka:** विहाय कामान्यः सर्वान्पुमांश्चरति निःस्पृहः। निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः स शान्तिमधिगच्छति॥ (vihāya kāmān yaḥ sarvān pumāṁś carati niḥspṛhaḥ, nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ sa śāntim adhigacchati)
   - **Meaning:** One who gives up selfish cravings and lives free of "mine" (*nirmama*) and free of ego (*nirahaṅkāra*) finds peace.
   - **In practice:** Not "leave everything and follow dharma", but "do everything, own nothing in the ego". Keep every duty (family, work, relationships), and let go of clutching at "mine" and "me" while doing it. At work: the client's win matters more than whose code it was.
   - *Added: Oct 18, 2026*
